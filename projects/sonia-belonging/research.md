# Research: Sonia Gandhi, *Belonging: A Journey of Love*

Researched 10 Nov 2026, launch day. **Source base (owner's decision):** published excerpts, her interviews and news
reports only. The full book is a paid release, and we have not read it. Every factual line is in `claims.csv`.

## What everyone says vs what the excerpts show
- **Everyone:** "7 shocking revelations" lists: Indira's temper, Maneka, Modi, Emergency, why she refused PM.
- **What the excerpts show:** one thread runs through them, the family's fear of the PM chair: 1984 (she begs Rajiv),
  1991 (Rahul's warning), 2004 (the children stop her). That is our film.

## The uploaded draft: what we dropped and why
The draft we were sent (`3ee672b1-Claude.md`) had lines we could not verify or that were wrong:
- **"Her first child, a son, died on the day he was born": not found in any source.** Facebook news posts mention "a
  miscarriage during her first pregnancy", but they credit it to an author they call "he", not to her memoir. The
  likeliest candidate is Javier Moro's dramatised 2008 novel *The Red Sari* (Indian Express explainer:
  https://indianexpress.com/article/explained/explained-politics/sonia-gandhi-book-controversy-red-sari-memoir-10857290),
  but we have not confirmed that. **It stays out of the film.**
- "She met Rajiv in a restaurant": true, The Varsity, a small Greek restaurant in Cambridge (kept, sourced).
- "The book answers criticism / is a political strategy": a question we put to the viewer, giving both sides.
- Its "..." pauses, the opening greeting style and the subscribe-only ending were replaced by our house rules.
