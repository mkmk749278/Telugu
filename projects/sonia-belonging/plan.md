# Sonia Gandhi's *Belonging*: the plan

**Channel:** Be Practical with Kishore Telugu, first film. **Released:** the book came out on 10 Nov 2026; this film rides launch week.
**Fact base (owner, 2026-11-10):** published excerpts and interviews only. Every line is credited on screen to the paper that printed it.

## The promise (locked)
**One sentence:** her own book tells the night Sonia Gandhi said *yes* to being Prime Minister in 2004, and why she took it back.

**Title (main):** `Sonia Gandhi PM ఎందుకు కాలేదు? ఆమె పుస్తకంలోనే సమాధానం`
Alternatives, same promise:
- `ఆ రాత్రి సోనియా "సరే" అన్నారు | Belonging Book`
- `Sonia Gandhi Book: PM పదవి వద్దన్న ఆ రాత్రి`

**Thumbnail (main):** the book cover on a dark table, a hand-drawn window frame behind it with a girl's silhouette at the window (illustration, not a real person). Words: **ఒకసారి "సరే"** in caution yellow. Bottom-right empty.
Challengers (same promise): (a) the 2004 Central Hall press photo (licensed) + **PM వద్దు** in red; (b) the book + an open window graphic + **ఆ రాత్రి ఏమైంది?**
Sentence 1 ("సోనియా గాంధీ, ప్రైమ్ మినిస్టర్ పదవికి ఒకసారి సరే అన్నారు.") confirms all three.

## Our edge
Every channel is listing "7 revelations". We tell **one thread** the excerpts reveal: the same fear of that chair passing down a family.
1984 she begs Rajiv with folded hands ("They will kill me anyway"); 1991 Rahul warns about his father's security; 2004 her children stop her.
And we are the only Telugu film that gives the critics' side (BJP, AAP, the Penguin dispute) on screen with sources.

## Angles considered
1. *The night she said yes* (chosen): a single scene, a reversal, a family thread; the most searched question (why she refused PM).
2. *7 revelations*: what everyone is making; no story, no edge.
3. *The Penguin censorship row*: contested, thin on facts both sides can verify.

## Cold opens considered
1. The conference room: "Theek hai", the open window, Priyanka (chosen: inside a real moment, sets the open loop).
2. Oct 31 1984 hospital: "They will kill me anyway" (kept as the mid-film turn).
3. Hyderabad bookshops on launch morning (kept for the landing: local relevance).
