## 1. Venkat Reddy, 67, retired irrigation chief engineer

**a)** The first sentence is exactly the thumbnail. After 30 seconds I know the story: a barrage that passed its design flood sank on a dry day, four years old. I stay to catch mistakes.

**b)** P1 L, P2 L, P3 L, P4 N, P5 L, P6 L (wincing), P7 N, P8 L, P9 N, P10 D, P11 L, P12 N. I do not leave.

**c)**
1. Sand washed out from under the Block 7 raft, the raft lost support, and the pier went down 1.25 m.
2. The report says piping through defective secant-pile cutoffs, with the downstream protection lost in 2019 and never repaired, and water stored like a dam.
3. The other side says sabotage (BRS), a verdict given without seeing the foundation (the department), and "we built to the given design" (L&T).

**d)** "ఇక్కడి వరకు పాఠం" tells me I was being lectured. The rest is good spoken Telugu.

**e)** Technical statements that are wrong or too absolute:
- **Barrage function.** "దీని పని ఒక్కటే. నదిని ఆపి, నీళ్ళని నిలబెట్టడం" is wrong, and you contradict it yourself in P8: "డ్యామ్ లాగా… నీళ్ళు నిలవ చేసేది కాదు".
- **The ladder.** "మొదటి మెట్టు విరిగితే… కింద నుంచి ఎక్కడానికి దారి ఉండదు" is too absolute. As I remember it, Yellampalli also gets its own river water. BRS people say exactly this, and you hand them the rebuttal.
- **Concrete blocks.** "ఆ వేగాన్ని చంపడానికి… కాంక్రీట్ బ్లాక్స్ పరుస్తారు" is wrong. The stilling basin kills the energy; the blocks stop the bed scouring beyond it. In P11 you use "నీళ్ళ వేగాన్ని చంపే నేల" for a different thing.
- **Cutoff.** "ఇసుక కదలదు" is too absolute. "ప్రమాదం… ఎంత వేగంగా బయటికి వస్తున్నాయి" is an acceptable simplification of exit gradient.
- **Rigid structure.** "నేల మీద, రాయికి ఆనిన కట్టడం" overstates it. The cutoffs touch rock; the raft still sits on sand, and that is the whole problem.
- **Uplift.** "నీటి ఒత్తిడి మారిపోతుంది" does not say whether it goes up or down, and you never use it again.
- **The gap in the logic.** If both walls go to rock, where did the sand go out? You never show the exit path.
- **Schedule.** Work starts in December with a June 2027 target, for a diaphragm wall along 1.6 km. You read that out without a raised eyebrow.

**h)** Open question: did the 2022 flood start the damage? I would repeat: "కట్టడం కనిపించేది పైన. నిలబడేది కింద."

# Panel

## 1. Against the existing videos

**What they already do better**
- marketfeed owns the money and data. Your P10 is a thinner version of it, and it is where three of us drifted or left.
- Kranthi has a face and anger, which is why partisans share him.
- Arun Surya Teja's 2019 film explains the whole lift system better than your one-minute P4.
- BBC Telugu has farmers and the site on camera. Your film has no human voice except a quoted challenge.

**What only this film does**
- It goes under the raft: బుంగ, cutoff, the pencil wall, floating versus rigid, and the 2019 downstream damage, in an order a 10th-class farmer followed.
- It gives the department, L&T and the sabotage claim a real hearing.
- It admits what it could not find.

That is your whole reason to exist, so protect it and trim what others do better.

## 2. Top 10 fixes, in order of impact

**1. The film contradicts itself on what the committee saw (P3 against P9).**
