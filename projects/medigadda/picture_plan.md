## The look (this film only)
**A site engineer's drafting table in evening light.** Warm drafting paper with a faint 10 mm grid, a steel scale,
pencils and a rolled drawing at the edges; the barrage cross-section stands on it as a cut model (concrete grey,
sand ochre, rock dark basalt, water a clear blue, the void black). One accent each: **caution yellow** for what the
voice points to, **orange** for the sunk pier, **blue arrows** for water, **ochre dots** for sand grains. No party
colour anywhere (no pink, no green-white-saffron bands); politicians appear only as names in type, never as faces.
Telugu labels in Noto Sans Telugu, numbers in Inter Tight; every label 2–4 words.

**The motif, and each return:** the row of pencils (the secant-pile wall). 1: real pencils on the table become the
wall (s118). 2: one gap, and water finds it (s120–123). 3: the gap inside the cutaway, sand leaving (s177–179).
4: the pencils replaced by one poured wall (s313–314). 5: the last frame, the pencils back in their tray beside six
borehole pins (s345).
**The sound motif:** one low thud at s7; the same thud, softer, when the raft settles at s191.
**The staircase** (first step): drawn at s37–39, returns on the rupee coin at s270 and whole again at s338.
