# Medigadda: why Block 7 sank (the plan)

**Channel:** Be Practical with Kishore Telugu. First film of the new direction (PLAYBOOK §1). **State:** Telangana.

## The promise (to be locked by the owner)
**One sentence:** the dam-safety report says the sand under Medigadda's concrete was washed away and left an empty
space; this film takes you under the raft and shows how, with the report's own pages and every side's answer.

**Title (main):** `మేడిగడ్డ ఎందుకు కుంగింది? రాఫ్ట్ కింద ఖాళీ | Medigadda Barrage`
Alternatives, same promise:
- `85 బోర్లు వెయ్యాల్సిన చోట 6 | Medigadda ఎందుకు కుంగింది?`
- `Medigadda ఎందుకు కుంగింది? ₹1.47 లక్షల కోట్లు దాటొచ్చన్న ప్రాజెక్ట్, రాఫ్ట్ కింద ఖాళీ`

The independent review's BRS-leaning viewer distrusted the film from a title that printed ₹1.47 lakh crore as fact
(it is CAG's "likely to exceed" for the whole Kaleshwaram project). The main title therefore carries no figure; the
rupee version is an alternative for the owner, worded as CAG worded it.

**Thumbnail (main):** a bright cutaway of Block 7 on a drafting table: the raft and piers in concrete grey, pier 20
sunk and glowing orange, a dark empty pocket under the raft with blue water arrows carrying sand through a gap in the
cutoff wall. Words: **రాఫ్ట్ కింద ఖాళీ** in caution yellow. Bottom-right empty. No politician, no party colour.
Challengers: (a) the same cutaway from the downstream side + **ఇసుక ఎక్కడ పోయింది?**; (b) two numbers side by side,
85 and 6, over the barrage photo + **85 కి 6**.
Sentence 1 ("మేడిగడ్డ బ్యారేజ్ కింద, కాంక్రీట్ కి ఇసుకకి మధ్య, ఖాళీ ఏర్పడింది.") confirms all three; challenger (b) is
confirmed by the boreholes beat, which must then be named in the first 45 seconds if (b) is chosen.

## Our edge
Every existing Telugu film tells the whole Kaleshwaram project, its money or its politics, to camera. **None goes
under the barrage.** We show the mechanism (seepage, piping, the cutoff wall with a gap) as one continuous cutaway,
with the dam-safety reports as the spine and four other voices answered on screen (the sabotage complaint, the
department, L&T, the High Court).

## Angles considered
1. **Under the raft** (chosen): one mechanism, one evening, one report; nobody has it.
2. *The 52-paise project*: the money story; marketfeed telugu 2.0 covered it nine days ago (3.6 lakh views).
3. *Sabotage or failure?*: a debate format; pulls the film into parties and leaves the viewer with no answer.

## Cold opens considered
1. The engineer on the bridge at dusk, the sound, the sunk parapet (chosen: a real person in a real moment).
2. July 2022: the barrage under its biggest flood, standing (kept as the paradox at 0:25).
3. A village tank bund with a muddy leak (kept as the picture from the viewer's life in chapter 2).

## Endings considered
1. The saying, "కట్టడం కనిపించేది పైన. నిలబడేది కింద.", then three dates to watch (chosen).
2. The farmer waiting for water (no sourced person yet; needs research before use).
3. The bill: who pays (unsettled today; kept as a beat, not the ending).

## Creative spine
- **The image the viewer keeps:** the row of pencils with one gap, and water finding it.
- **Motif:** "the first step" (మొదటి మెట్టు) of a staircase that lifts a river; returns at the money and at the end.
- **Sound:** the evening's single low thud returns, quieter, when the raft settles in the set piece.
- **Retell line:** "85 బోర్లు వెయ్యాల్సిన చోట 6 వేశారు."
- **Devices by chapter:** a moment (open), a map and a staircase (what it is), a home comparison (how barrages
  stand), document walk (what was different), one continuous cutaway (the set piece), four voices (the other side),
  a rupee brought to an acre (money), a calendar (today).

## Fairness flags
- NDSA's findings are NDSA's; several details reach us through news reports of the final report, and the narration
  says so. **The report PDFs must be read before the fact-check closes** (`claims.csv` marks each such row).
- The sabotage complaint is real and the BRS position is stated in its own terms; we say no public investigation
  report on that angle was found.
- L&T: its statement and its letter are given; no finding of guilt is stated.
- The High Court order of 22 April 2026 is stated narrowly: commission valid, observations inoperative, no action.
- Both governments: the previous one for design, haste and maintenance as the reports say; the present one for the
  grouting before tests, the delay and the unpaid bills.
- No party colours; no politician's face in the thumbnail.
