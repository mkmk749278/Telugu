## The barrage (Wikipedia, to re-source)
Medigadda village, Mahadevpur mandal, Jayashankar Bhupalpally district; on the Godavari at the Maharashtra border;
1,632 m long, 85 gates; design discharge 28.25 lakh cusecs; pond level 100 m, bed 89 m; 16.17 TMC; built by L&T;
cost ₹1,849 crore (check); foundation 2 May 2016 (uncited); opened 21 June 2019. July 2022 flood about 28.7–28.8 lakh
cusecs, above the design figure; Kannepally pump house submerged.

## The evening (news, Oct 2023)
- 21 Oct 2023, evening: a loud sound, reported at about 6:20 pm by an assistant executive engineer on duty; piers 19,
  20, 21 of Block 7 damaged, pier 20 the worst; the road bridge sagged. About 356 m from the Maharashtra end.
- Barrage officials filed a complaint at Mahadevpur police suspecting sabotage; a case against unknown persons. The
  Engineer-in-Chief said no conspiracy, sand movement under a pier. (The South First, Telangana Today, TNM.)
- By 24 Oct the pier had settled 1.256 m (The South First on the NDSA final report).
- Weeks before the Telangana assembly election (30 Nov 2023): handle with care.

## NDSA committee, first report (1 Nov 2023; SANDRP has excerpts)
- Six-member panel under Anil Jain, formed 22 Oct, site 23–27 Oct.
- "due to a combination of issues involving planning, design, quality control and Operation and maintenance"
- "The Barrage has been designed as a floating structure but constructed as a rigid structure."
- "The barrage under the present condition is rendered useless until fully rehabilitated."
- "the likelihood of the failure of other blocks resulting in a similar mode exists."
- "The dam owners have not inspected or maintained the cement concrete blocks or launching aprons" since 2019-20.
- The state's reply (4 Nov 2023): findings "either unsubstantiated or made without a full appreciation of the facts";
  the cause can be known only after the foundation is inspected; admitted the 2023 pre-monsoon inspection "could not
  be carried out".

## NDSA final report (dated 24 April 2025; via The South First, Deccan Chronicle, Siasat)
- Piping under the raft: sand washed out, cavities formed; settlement of the raft the primary cause.
- Secant-pile cutoffs: weak quality control; upstream and downstream cutoffs not properly joined to the raft; sheet
  piles had been replaced with secant piles; keys under the piers removed.
- Six boreholes drilled against 85 recommended by CBIP guidelines; DPR prepared in four months.
- Model studies missed cases such as a single gate cracked open at pond level; hydraulic jumps washed out downstream
  aprons and CC blocks from 2019; wire gabions replaced with loose rip-rap.
- Barrages used to store water like dams; never emptied for repair; no O&M manual; inspections not done.
- Annaram and Sundilla show similar seepage and piping distress; their sites were moved after the agreements.
- Block 7: irreversible damage; rebuild or stabilise without harming neighbours. Grouting done before NDSA's tests
  changed the ground conditions.
- "The worst man-made disaster ever in the history of India": The South First attributes the words to the report;
  **verify in the report itself before use.**
- **Need the report PDF itself** (not found online yet): ask the owner's Cowork to fetch it from the Telangana
  irrigation department or NDSA site.

## CAG (Performance Audit on Kaleshwaram, tabled Feb 2024; cag.gov.in, executive summary PDF downloaded but not yet read)
- Cost likely to exceed ₹1,47,427.41 crore against ₹81,911.01 crore projected to CWC.
- BCR 0.52 at the latest cost ("every rupee spent on the project would yield only 52 paise"); 0.75 even at the lower
  cost; the state had projected about 1.51.
- These are for the whole project, not the barrage.

## Commission, court, both sides
- Justice P.C. Ghose Commission: 665-page report, 31 July 2025; held the then CM responsible (News On AIR, 4 Aug 2025).
- Telangana High Court, 22 April 2026: the commission validly constituted, but its observations "shall remain
  inoperative" and no action on its findings (Section 8B safeguards not followed). Check for any appeal since.
- BRS position: one pier of one block; repairable; sabotage alleged, SIT demanded; the barrage passed floods every
  year since (5.35 lakh cusecs with all 85 gates open, Oct 2025; Telangana Today).
- Congress government position: design and execution failure of the previous government (minister Uttam Kumar Reddy).
- L&T: the commission held it responsible for defect rectification; the irrigation department says L&T failed on
  quality (Deccan Chronicle); **L&T's own published answer still to collect.**

## Where it stands (re-check within 24 h of upload)
- 5 Nov 2026 (Telangana Today): the CM asked for final approvals by the end of November and work to start in
  December (earlier target mid-November); proposals for the three barrages about ₹4,193 crore (an earlier report said
  about ₹2,500 crore, Medigadda over ₹700 crore); a central expert committee gave in-principle approval; contractors
  to pay for structural defects, the state for additional works; engineers say lifting water at Medigadda is not
  feasible now.
- Minister's target: Medigadda fully operational by June 2027 (NewsMeter).
- Design: diaphragm walls at all three barrages and longer stilling basins (reported); Block 7: strengthen or
  dismantle and rebuild, undecided.
