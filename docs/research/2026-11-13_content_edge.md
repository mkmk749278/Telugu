## 1. The audit's verdict

| Category | Score /50 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **B. Regional industrial forensics and mega-infrastructure autopsies** | **46** | **The lead format** |
| C. Corporate autopsies, white-collar fraud, high finance | 43 | Second; enters inside B films first (project finance, contractor insolvency, benefit-cost ratio), separate films later |
| A. Geopolitics and intelligence | 37 | No: leans on memoirs and archive footage, near the synthetic-content line |
| D. 20th-century political and historical turning points | 35 | No: party legacies, comment wars, archive footage we do not own |

Why B wins, in the audit's words: Telugu viewers get TV-news shouting, devotional and motivation channels, trivia
lists and hour-long podcasts; educated viewers leave for English and Hindi channels for calm analysis; the big projects
of the two states (Kaleshwaram/Medigadda, Polavaram, the SLBC tunnel, Pinnapuram) fill the news but video rarely goes
past a three-minute report or a party accusation. Our stack (Blender cutaways, Remotion data, primary documents) is
exactly what that subject needs.

The audit's other points we adopt:
- **The TV in the living room.** Kantar: 166 million monthly connected-TV viewers in India in Q1 2026. The Media Ant's
  Reach Planner model (Aug 2026): Andhra Pradesh 10.10 million reachable CTV viewers; rural Telangana 32.2%
  penetration, the highest in that dataset. Build every frame for a 55-inch TV and a family watching together.
- **The diaspora.** 605,544 people aged five and over speak Telugu at home in the US (2024 American Community Survey;
  733,527 in Telugu-language households; press figures of 1.23 million use a wider measure). US long-form RPM in
  creator calculators is several times India's. The audit's blended RPM of ₹210–313 per 1,000 views assumes 10–15% US
  viewers and calculator rates: **an estimate to test in Studio, never a plan figure.**
- **Our own footage only.** India's Section 52 fair dealing is narrow (criticism or review, reporting current events);
  TV-news and film clips are a takedown risk. Original 3D, our own graphics and short quoted excerpts of public
  reports keep us clear. This lowers the risk; it is not immunity: every photo still needs its licence.
- **AI disclosure.** Clearly unrealistic animation (cutaways, CAD-style renders, data graphics) needs no "altered
  content" label. A realistic scene of a real event or place does.
- **Voice.** No independent listening test of Telugu TTS engines exists; the audit asks for an A/B between ElevenLabs
  and Sarvam Bulbul v3 on technical Tenglish paragraphs before the pilot voice is locked (the owner's decision).

### 2.3 Things the audit could not know
- **Competition is arriving.** marketfeed telugu 2.0 put out a 27-minute Kaleshwaram data film on 4 Nov 2026 (358K).
  The Medigadda film must be the mechanism under the raft, not the project's money story again.
- **SLBC has moved on.** The audit ends at "the last 50 metres could not be crossed". In October 2026 the minister
  said the tunnel had crossed the shear zone on a realigned path with steel supports, about 79% complete, target
  mid-2028 (Deccan Chronicle, Hans India). The film ends on today's status and the date to watch.
- **Pinnapuram has the weakest search demand of the three pilots** and its "world's first" line is the developer's
  and a minister's wording, not an independent ranking; "gravity battery" is our phrase for pumped storage, and CEA
  lists show the units commissioned in stages through 2025. It is still the good-news film that balances two failure
  stories and gives Andhra Pradesh a film; its package needs a stronger household hook (the evening power cut, the
  current bill) than the project's name.
- **Shorts carry these topics far** (1.5M to 6.6M on a diaphragm wall, a TBM, the CAG report). The audit is right that
  Shorts pay almost nothing; they are how a channel with one subscriber gets found.
- **The live topic this month is Hyderabad's water** (water board emergency declared, shortages expected from
  December). Three channels have covered it already (1.9M, 383K, 371K); it shows what a live regional question does.
